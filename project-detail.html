    Corporate Scent Contracting

    A Complete Scenting Solution for Your Business

    One monthly contract covers everything your space needs to smell its best: a scent profile shaped around your brand, professional diffuser installation, regular rotation and ongoing consultations, all managed by our team.

    The service typically includes the following aspects:

    • Customized Scent Profiles

      Tailoring scents to match the brand personality and objectives of the commercial firm, ensuring a unique and recognizable aroma that complements the overall customer experience.

    • Regular Scent Rotation

      Implementing a schedule for changing or rotating scents based on seasons, promotions, or specific events. This keeps the atmosphere fresh and engaging for both customers and employees.

    • Scent Diffuser Installation

      Deploying professional-grade scent diffusers strategically throughout the commercial space to evenly distribute the chosen fragrance, ensuring a consistent and pleasant environment.

    • Scent Consultation and Adjustments

      Regular consultations to assess the effectiveness of the chosen scents and make adjustments based on feedback, seasonal changes, or any evolving requirements of the commercial firm.

    • Brand Alignment

      Ensuring that the selected scents align with the brand values, target audience, and the specific goals of the commercial firm, contributing to a cohesive and immersive brand experience.

    • Quality Assurance

      Providing high-quality fragrance oils and reliable scent diffusion technology to maintain a consistent and long-lasting scent experience.

    • Cost-Effective Solutions

      Offering a monthly contract provides a cost-effective approach for commercial firms, allowing them to budget efficiently while benefiting from a professionally managed scent marketing strategy.
