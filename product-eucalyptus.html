    Aromatic Oil · Citrus

    Eucalyptus

    Price AED 175

    "Eucalyptus" Aromatic Oil is derived from the leaves of the eucalyptus tree, primarily Eucalyptus globulus. Known for its invigorating and refreshing aroma, eucalyptus oil has a range of therapeutic properties that have made it a popular choice in aromatherapy and holistic health practices. Its a fresh, camphoraceous scent with subtle undertones of sweetness. The aroma is often described as crisp, clean, and invigorating, making it a favorite for respiratory and spa applications.
