    Refund and Returns Policy

    We want you to be happy with every ATMOCARE purchase. This policy explains when returns, replacements and refunds apply, and how to request one.

    Last updated 16 October 2026

    01Overview

    Return and refund requests are reviewed case by case, in line with this policy and applicable consumer protection requirements.

    02Return Eligibility

    To be considered for a return, products should generally be:

    • Unused and in the same condition in which they were received.
    • In their original packaging, with all accessories, manuals and parts included.
    • Accompanied by the order number or proof of purchase.

    03Non-Returnable Items

    For hygiene and safety reasons, opened or used aromatic oils and refills may not be eligible for return unless they are damaged, defective or incorrect. Custom fragrances, made-to-order items and installed scent contracting work may also be excluded, where legally permitted.

    04Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

    If you receive a product that is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible after delivery. We will work with you to resolve the issue promptly.

    05How to Request a Return

    To request a return, contact our support team and include:

    • Your order number and the name used on the order.
    • The product(s) you would like to return and the reason for the request.
    • Clear photographs or other supporting information, where applicable.

    Our team will review the request and advise whether a return, replacement, repair, or refund is appropriate.

    06Refunds

    Refunds are issued once the returned product has been received and inspected, or once the request has otherwise been approved.

    Once approved, refunds will be processed through the original payment method wherever possible, or through another legally permitted method agreed upon where necessary.

    The time taken for the refund to appear in your account may depend on your bank or payment provider.

    07Return Shipping Costs

    Where a return results from an incorrect, damaged, or defective product supplied by ATMOCARE, the handling of return shipping costs will be determined in accordance with the applicable policy and consumer protection requirements.

    For other return requests, customers may be responsible for return shipping costs where legally permitted and clearly communicated.

    08Replacements and Exchanges

    Where a replacement or exchange is approved, it will be subject to product availability. If the same product is unavailable, we may offer an alternative or a refund.

    09Order Cancellations

    If you wish to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible. Orders can usually be cancelled before they are dispatched; once an order has been dispatched, it will be treated as a return request.

    If a cancelled order has already been paid for, the refund will be processed as described above.
