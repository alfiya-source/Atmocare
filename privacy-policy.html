    Privacy Policy

    This Privacy Policy explains how ATMOCARE (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit our website, place an order, or contact us about our products and services.

    01Introduction

    We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. By using our website or services, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

    02Information We Collect

    We may collect the following information when you interact with us:

    • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number and billing or shipping address.
    • Order details, including the products purchased, order value and delivery preferences.
    • Messages, enquiries or requirements you send us through our contact forms, email or WhatsApp.
    • Technical information such as browser type, device, IP address and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

    03How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect to:

    • Process, confirm and deliver your orders.
    • Respond to enquiries, quotation requests and support requests.
    • Provide scent contracting, installation and after-sales services.
    • Improve our website, products and customer experience.
    • Send promotional communications where permitted and applicable.
    • Meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

    04Sharing Your Information

    We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, such as delivery partners, payment providers and IT or hosting services, only to the extent needed for them to perform those services.

    We do not authorize these service providers to use your information beyond the purposes for which it is shared, subject to applicable arrangements and legal requirements.

    We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of ATMOCARE, our customers or others.

    05Cookies

    Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart, remember your preferences and understand how the website is used. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings; some features of the website may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

    06Payment Security

    Payments made through our website may be processed by authorized third-party payment providers. Payment information is handled according to the applicable provider's security practices and policies.

    07Data Retention and Security

    We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse; however, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

    08Third-Party Links

    Our website may contain links to external websites or services. ATMOCARE is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage users to review their respective privacy policies.

    09Your Rights

    Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may opt out of promotional communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us.

    10Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
